Kleinbaum and Klein (2012), Survival Analysis: A Self-Learning Text

The book whose cover looks similar to the ISLP cover, but is exclusively focused on Survival Analysis models – also has a two-column pictures-and-text format specifically aimed at self-learning for this topic.
